BABU — cart + arithmetic
========================
ONE file. Upload babu-js.txt to repo ROOT, rename to:  babu.js
Then Ctrl+Shift+R.

NEW: he can now add things to the cart and work out prices.
  "add 2kg nguru"                -> puts it in the cart, shows the running total
  "nataka kilo mbili za changu"  -> same, in Swahili
  "how much for 3kg nguru"       -> KES 3,600, and offers to add it
