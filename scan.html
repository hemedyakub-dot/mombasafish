Keep it fresh

Hifadhi vizuri — four rules for the best taste

  • 1
    Into the cold straight away. Fridge (0–4°C): cook within 2 days. Freezer (−18°C): best within 3 months.
  • 2
    Thaw in the fridge overnight, or in a sealed bag in cold water for a quick thaw. Never on the counter.
  • 3
    Don't refreeze once thawed — cook it and enjoy it.
  • 4
    Fresh fish smells of the sea, not "fishy". Anything not right? Tell us on WhatsApp.
