Nutritional values are approximate, per 100 g raw edible portion, and vary with size and season. This is general information, not medical or dietary advice.

How to cook it

Fry whole and crisp. Mkundaji has a distinctive, almost shellfish-like sweetness that is lost if you smother it in spice.

Frying Grilling

Goes well with: Salt, lime, chilli, fried cassava

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Keep on ice or in the coldest part of the fridge (0–4 °C) and cook within 2 days of delivery. To freeze, pat dry, wrap tightly and freeze at −18 °C; use within 3 months. Never refreeze fish that has thawed.

Keeps: 2 days refrigerated · 3 months frozen

Questions

Why is mkundaji special?

It has a sweet, almost prawn-like flavour that no other fish on the slab matches — a chef's fish.

What size are they?

Small — usually 150–400 g each, so several to a kilo.
