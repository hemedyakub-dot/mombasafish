How to cook it

Grill or bake whole with nothing more than salt, lime and oil — snapper is a fish that rewards restraint. Also excellent pan-fried in fillets, skin on, for crisp skin.

Storage & shelf life

Keep on ice or in the coldest part of the fridge (0–4 °C) and cook within 2 days of delivery. To freeze, pat dry, wrap tightly and freeze at −18 °C; use within 3 months. Never refreeze fish that has thawed.

Questions

Why is snapper more expensive filleted?

Filleting a whole snapper yields roughly 40–45% of its live weight in boneless meat, so one kilo of fillet takes over two kilos of whole fish, plus the labour of cutting it.

Skin on or off?

Your choice — say which at checkout. Skin on is better for pan-frying and grilling; skin off suits curries and stews.

Is it really red?

The skin is rose-pink to red; the flesh is white and firm. Colour fades slightly with time on ice, which is normal.
