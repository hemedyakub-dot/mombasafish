Storage & shelf life

Keep on ice or in the coldest part of the fridge (0–4 °C) and cook within 2 days of delivery. To freeze, pat dry, wrap tightly and freeze at −18 °C; use within 3 months. Never refreeze fish that has thawed.

Questions

Why is nguru the biryani fish?

Because it does not fall apart. The flesh is dense and holds together through the layering and steaming that biryani needs, where a softer fish would disintegrate into the rice.

Is nguru bony?

Very little. It has a central bone and no significant pin bones, so steaks are almost bone-free.

What is the usual size?

Nguru run large — often 3–8 kg whole. Most customers buy it cut into steaks. Tell us how many kilos and how you want it cut.
