How to cook it

Choose your whole snapper from the day's catch and we fillet it for you by hand.

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Fillets have no skin or bone to protect them. Keep at 0–4 °C, cook within 1–2 days, or freeze immediately at −18 °C for up to 3 months.

Keeps: 1–2 days refrigerated · 3 months frozen

Questions

How does this work?

You buy a whole snapper and we return it to you as boneless fillets. You are charged the filleted rate on the finished weight.

Do I get the frames back?

Yes if you want them — snapper heads and bones make an excellent stock or soup. Say so on WhatsApp.
