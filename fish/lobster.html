How to cook it

Split lengthwise, brush with garlic butter and grill shell-side down 6–8 minutes. Do not boil a good lobster to death — the tail is done the moment it turns opaque.

Goes well with: Garlic butter, lime, chilli, coconut rice

Questions

Is this the same as Maine lobster?

No — this is Indian Ocean spiny lobster, which has no large front claws. All the meat is in the tail, and it is sweeter than cold-water lobster.

How much per person?

One 400–500 g lobster per person as a main course.

Are they in season all year?

Availability varies with the monsoon and with closed-season rules. Message us for what is landing this week.
