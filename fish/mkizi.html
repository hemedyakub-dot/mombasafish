How to cook it

Fry it whole. Score the sides three times, salt, lime, and let the skin go properly crisp — that is where this fish is at its best. It also takes charcoal well. Gut it the moment it reaches you: mullet spoils from the inside faster than most, and quick gutting is the difference between a clean fish and a strong one.

Storage & shelf life

Keep on ice or in the coldest part of the fridge (0–4 °C) and cook within 2 days of delivery. To freeze, pat dry, wrap tightly and freeze at −18 °C; use within 3 months. Never refreeze fish that has thawed.

Questions

Is mkizi the same as red mullet?

No — they are not even related. Red mullet is a goatfish. Mkizi is a true mullet, family Mugilidae. Different fish, different flavour, different price.

Does mullet taste muddy?

It can, and we will not pretend otherwise — mullet feeds off the bottom and tastes of the water it lived in. Fish from clean inshore water is firm and clean-tasting. That is why we care where it comes from, and why we gut it straight away. If one ever reaches you tasting of mud, send a photograph and we will replace it.
