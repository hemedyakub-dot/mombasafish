Nutrition & health

~330 kcal · 60 g protein · 9 g fat · extremely high in calcium and protein by dry weight

Sun-drying concentrates everything. Weight for weight, dried anchovies are among the most protein- and calcium-dense foods you can keep in a cupboard.

How to cook it

Fry crisp as a snack, or use a handful to build savoury depth in a stew or vegetable dish. Rinse briefly first if you want less salt.

Goes well with: Ugali, sukuma wiki, tomato stew

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Store in a sealed container in a cool, dry, dark place. No refrigeration needed.

Keeps: 6–12 months sealed and dry

Questions

Do they need refrigeration?

No. Sealed, cool and dry is enough. Refrigerate only if your kitchen is very humid.

How long do they keep?

Six to twelve months if properly sealed and kept dry.
