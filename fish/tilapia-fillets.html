How to cook it

Pan-fry, bake or stew. Tilapia is mild to the point of neutral, so it takes on whatever you cook it with.

Goes well with: Tomato stew, garlic, lemon, ugali

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Fillets have no skin or bone to protect them. Keep at 0–4 °C, cook within 1–2 days, or freeze immediately at −18 °C for up to 3 months.

Keeps: 1–2 days refrigerated · 3 months frozen

Questions

Is tilapia from the ocean?

No — tilapia is a freshwater fish. We say so clearly because everything else we sell is Indian Ocean and traceable to Shimoni.

Why is it cheaper than snapper?

Freshwater tilapia is farmed at scale and costs less to produce than a hand-caught reef fish.
