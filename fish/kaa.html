How to cook it

Steam or boil 12–15 minutes per kilo, then crack at the table. Also outstanding in a coconut curry, or grilled split with garlic butter.

Goes well with: Garlic butter, coconut, chilli, lime

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Shellfish are the most perishable thing we sell. Keep cold and cook the day of delivery where possible, and within 24 hours at the latest.

Keeps: 1 day refrigerated

Questions

Are they live?

We supply them fresh and iced. If you want live crab for a specific event, ask on WhatsApp and we will try to arrange it.

How much crab per person?

Allow 400–500 g of whole crab per person as a main course — there is a lot of shell.

What is mud crab?

Scylla serrata — the big-clawed mangrove crab of the East African coast, and the finest eating crab in these waters.
