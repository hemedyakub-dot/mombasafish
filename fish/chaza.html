Questions

Are they safe to eat raw?

Raw shellfish always carries some risk. Ours are gathered and iced fresh, but anyone pregnant, immunocompromised, elderly or very young should eat oysters cooked rather than raw.

How many per person?

Six to twelve as a starter.

How do I know one is bad?

Discard any that is already open and does not close when tapped, or that smells of anything but clean sea.
