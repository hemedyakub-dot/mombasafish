How to cook it

Pan-fry skin-side down in a hot dry pan for crisp skin, or bake with lime and herbs. Thin fillets need 3–4 minutes total.

Goes well with: Lime, garlic, herbs, salad, rice

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Fillets have no skin or bone to protect them. Keep at 0–4 °C, cook within 1–2 days, or freeze immediately at −18 °C for up to 3 months.

Keeps: 1–2 days refrigerated · 3 months frozen

Checked before the box is closed

Every fish is looked over by hand before it is packed. These are the four things we check, and they are the same four you should check when it reaches you.

  • EyesClear and full. Never cloudy or sunken.
  • GillsLifted and looked at. Deep red, not brown or grey.
  • FleshPressed with a finger. It springs back.
  • SmellClean sea. Never ammonia.

Questions

Why is fillet three times the whole price?

Because one kilo of fillet takes over two kilos of whole fish — filleting yields about 40–45% — plus the labour of cutting it by hand.
