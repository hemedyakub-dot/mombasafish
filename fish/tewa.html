How to cook it

The mchuzi wa samaki classic. Large white flakes that soak up a coconut-and-tomato curry. Also good fried, though it is almost too lean for the grill.

Curry Frying

Goes well with: Coconut curry, tomato, coriander, ugali

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Keep on ice or in the coldest part of the fridge (0–4 °C) and cook within 2 days of delivery. To freeze, pat dry, wrap tightly and freeze at −18 °C; use within 3 months. Never refreeze fish that has thawed.

Keeps: 2 days refrigerated · 3 months frozen

Questions

Why is tewa good for curry?

It flakes into large, clean pieces that absorb sauce rather than dissolving into it.

Is it bony?

Few bones and a simple structure — easy eating.
