Questions

Why does mangrove honey taste different?

Because the bees forage mangrove blossom, which gives a darker, richer honey with a faint salt-marsh note you will not find in an inland honey.

Does beekeeping help the mangroves?

Yes — it gives coastal communities an income that depends on the forest staying standing rather than being cut for timber or charcoal.

Will it crystallise?

Raw honey often does. Stand the jar in warm water to bring it back — never microwave it.
