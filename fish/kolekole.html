How to cook it

Handles bold seasoning better than almost anything else on the slab. Deep-fry in a spiced crust, grill over charcoal, or cook long in a heavily spiced curry.

Frying Grilling Curry

Goes well with: Pilipili, tamarind, heavy spice, coconut

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Keep on ice or in the coldest part of the fridge (0–4 °C) and cook within 2 days of delivery. To freeze, pat dry, wrap tightly and freeze at −18 °C; use within 3 months. Never refreeze fish that has thawed.

Keeps: 2 days refrigerated · 3 months frozen

Questions

Is kolekole strong-tasting?

Stronger than changu or tewa, milder than tuna. It is the fish to choose if you find white fish bland.

Good for grilling?

Excellent. The dense flesh holds together on a grate where softer fish break up.
