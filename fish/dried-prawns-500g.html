How to cook it

Soak 15 minutes in warm water, then add to curries, pilau, coconut stews or vegetables. The soaking water is full of flavour — use it.

Goes well with: Coconut stew, pilau, sukuma wiki, mchuzi

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Airtight container, cool and dry, away from sunlight. If your kitchen is humid, keep them in the fridge.

Keeps: 6 months sealed and dry

Questions

Do I have to soak them?

For stews and curries, yes — 15 minutes in warm water. To use as a crunchy garnish, fry them dry.

How long do they keep?

Six months sealed and dry, longer in the fridge.
