How to cook it

Treat it like tuna. Thick steaks, seared hot and fast or grilled over charcoal, served on the rare side of done. It dries out quickly if overcooked.

Grilling Raw / sashimi

Goes well with: Lime, black pepper, charred onion

Every order is cleaned to your preference — scaled, gutted, filleted, cut into steaks or left whole. Choose at checkout.

Storage & shelf life

Keep on ice or in the coldest part of the fridge (0–4 °C) and cook within 2 days of delivery. To freeze, pat dry, wrap tightly and freeze at −18 °C; use within 3 months. Never refreeze fish that has thawed.

Keeps: 2 days refrigerated · 3 months frozen

Questions

Is sailfish like tuna?

Similar texture and steak cut, slightly paler and leaner. Cook it the same way and do not overdo it.

Can it be eaten raw?

We do not recommend it for billfish. Cook sailfish through or to medium at least.
