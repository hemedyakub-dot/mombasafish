One name · Eight pillars

The Mombasafish ecosystem.

A fish shop is where it starts. An ocean economy is where it's going. These are the eight pillars we build on — each one already alive in what we do every day.

Every pillar lives inside one of our five branches — Market · Media · Academy · Ocean · Exports — and every branch stands on the same waters of Shimoni.

PILLAR 01 MOMBASAFISH MARKET

Fisheries

Seafood supply, market systems, and value chains — the engine of everything. We shorten the chain from boat to plate to almost nothing.

  • In practice today: same-day purchase from Shimoni's small boats — no brokers, no cold-storage limbo
  • 30+ species catalogued Swahili-first, priced per kg, ordered on WhatsApp, paid by M-Pesa
  • More of every shilling stays with the fishing families who caught it

See it live: the catalogue · From Boat to Box

PILLAR 02 MOMBASAFISH MEDIA

Ocean Media

Storytelling, documentaries, digital content, and awareness. The coast has stories the world hasn't heard — we tell them from the inside.

  • In practice today: the blog's four content pillars — Journey, Kitchen, Blue Economy, Product Guide
  • Weekly catch updates and fishermen stories across Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and X
  • Every FishBox carries a story: a boat, a name, a coastline

See it live: Our fish stories · YouTube

PILLAR 03 MOMBASAFISH MARKET · ACADEMY

Innovation

Technology, preservation, renewable energy, and new ideas — practical innovation that fits how the coast actually works, not imported templates.

  • In practice today: WhatsApp-first ordering — no cart forced on customers who buy by conversation
  • A live order tracker: every FishBox has an order number and code, trackable to the doorstep
  • Next: solar-powered cold storage and modern drying systems for the value chain

See it live: the order tracker · the interactive Fish Guide

PILLAR 04 MOMBASAFISH ACADEMY · OCEAN

Blue Economy

Education, advocacy, consulting, and sustainable ocean solutions. Kenya's coast feeds families and pays school fees — a healthy blue economy keeps it that way for the next generation.

  • In practice today: we covered the FAO's SOFIA 2026 report — launched right here in Mombasa — and what its numbers mean for Shimoni
  • Plain-language explainers on sustainable fishing for our customers
  • Western Indian Ocean small-scale fisheries support 65+ million people — we're one thread of that fabric

Read: What is the Blue Economy? · SOFIA 2026 in Mombasa

PILLAR 05 MOMBASAFISH MARKET · ACADEMY

Community

Fishermen, coastal livelihoods, culture, and empowerment. The business model itself is the community program: buy direct, pay fair, keep the money on the coast.

  • In practice today: direct daily trade with small-boat fishermen — their income doesn't leak to middlemen
  • Swahili leads on everything we publish — Jodari before Tuna, Pweza before Octopus. The culture is the brand
  • Next: Academy training for young coastal people — seafood handling, small-boat skills, digital trade

Read: why buying direct matters

PILLAR 06 MOMBASAFISH OCEAN

Marine Ecosystems

Reefs, mangroves, marine protected areas, and biodiversity conservation — the living systems every other pillar depends on.

  • In practice today: small-boat, low-impact methods only — no trawlers scraping the seabed we fish tomorrow
  • Asali ya Mikoko: our mangrove honey pays beekeepers to keep mangrove forests standing — nurseries of the ocean
  • We covered Kenya's biodegradable-net trials cutting "ghost fishing" — coastal innovation protecting coastal waters

Read: the ghost fishing story · mangrove honey

PILLAR 07 MOMBASAFISH MARKET · EXPORTS

Infrastructure

Cold chain, drying systems, solar solutions, and fisheries infrastructure — the unglamorous machinery that makes "fresh" a promise instead of a hope.

  • In practice today: EPS insulated coolers holding temperature up to 72 hours — Shimoni to 8 counties, chain unbroken
  • Same-day rider dispatch in Mombasa; express transport beyond
  • Next: solar cold rooms and traditional-modern drying systems at the landing site

See it live: cooler boxes · dispatch tracking

PILLAR 08 MOMBASAFISH EXPORTS

Trade & Exports

Taking the taste of the Swahili coast beyond Kenya — the same traceable, small-boat story, told to the world.

  • In practice today: export-grade species already in the catalogue — jumbo prawns, lobster, tuna, swordfish
  • Traceability is our export licence-in-waiting: every catch has a boat, a date, a coastline
  • Next: regional trade first, then international markets under the Mombasafish name
